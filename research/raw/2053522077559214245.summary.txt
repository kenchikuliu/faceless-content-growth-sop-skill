[header-two] 6. Voiceover and visual assembly
[unstyled] ElevenLabs is the entire voiceover layer. The free plan covers a couple of short videos so you can test the workflow. The $22 plan handles a full publishing schedule across all three platforms.
[unstyled] A practical tip nobody tells you: do not use the default ElevenLabs voices. Every faceless channel uses them and your audience knows. Clone a custom voice from a 30 second sample (royalty free voice acting marketplaces have these for under $10) and your channel sounds unique.
